LRU是面试必考题。HashMap保证O(1)查找，双向链表保证O(1)的插入/删除/移动到头部。两者结合实现完美的O(1)缓存。
LRU is a must-know interview topic. HashMap for O(1) lookup, doubly linked list for O(1) move-to-head. Together: perfect O(1) cache.
